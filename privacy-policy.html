Last updated: March 21, 2026

NomNom ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use our mobile application ("App").

We aim to collect as little data as possible.

a) Personal Information

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or contact details.

b) Usage Data

We may automatically collect limited non-personal information, including:

  • Device type and operating system

c) Advertising Data

We use third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob, which may collect:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information
  • Approximate location (non-precise)
  • Ad interaction data

2. How We Use Information

We use collected data to:

  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Fix bugs and technical issues
  • Analyze usage trends
  • Display advertisements

3. Third-Party Services

NomNom uses third-party services that may collect data independently, including:

  • Google AdMob (advertising)

These services operate under their own privacy policies.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data.

We may share limited data only:

  • With trusted third-party services (such as advertising providers)

5. Data Retention

We do not store personal data.

Any data collected by third-party services is managed according to their own policies.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location (e.g., EU/EEA), you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to data processing

To exercise these rights, please contact us.

7. Children’s Privacy

NomNom is a general audience app.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.
